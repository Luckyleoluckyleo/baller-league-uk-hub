## Nile Ranger: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Nile Ranger** is a striker for **N5 FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Newcastle United, Swindon Town, Southend United and Blackpool player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Nile Ranger is a striker who came through at **Newcastle United**, where he made his first-team debut as a teenager. Powerful and rapid, he went on to play for the likes of **Swindon Town**, **Southend United** and **Blackpool**.

At N5 FC he adds Championship and Premier League-level physicality to the attack — a handful for any defence in the compact 6v6 format.

## Career

Nile Ranger built his professional career at Newcastle United, Swindon Town, Southend United and Blackpool. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.
