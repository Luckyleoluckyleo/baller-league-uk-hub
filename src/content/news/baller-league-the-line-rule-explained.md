**The Line doubles any goal scored from in front of the offside line, in the attacking half.** For the duration of the window, long-range shooting stops being a low-percentage option and becomes the most valuable skill on the pitch.

It rewards distance rather than numbers — and the numbers say it changes shot selection more than it changes the scoreline.

## When The Line activates

The Line is a **second-half Gamechanger**, announced at the **27th minute** and live for a short window. Across Seasons 1–3 it has been activated **72 times** — 17.7% of all Gamechanger activations.

## Why doubling goals doesn't inflate the score

A rule that pays double for long-range goals sounds like a goal rush. It isn't. **32 of 72 Line windows produced no goals at all (44%)**, at **0.79 goals per activation** — the second-lowest of the six rules.

The explanation is simple once you look at what the rule actually rewards: hitting the target from distance is a specialist skill, and most players cannot produce it on demand. Doubling the value of a shot nobody takes does not create goals. What it does change is behaviour — players shoot from range earlier in possessions, sometimes instead of working a better chance closer in.

## How teams win The Line

- **Know who can hit it.** This is the one Gamechanger where an individual skill decides the window.
- **Shoot from range immediately.** A doubled goal from distance is worth the same as two close-range finishes.
- **Defend higher.** Pushing the defensive line up denies opponents the space in front of it where shots are worth double.
