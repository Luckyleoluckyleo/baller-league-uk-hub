## Goals, goals, goals

The Baller League is built for entertainment, and nothing captures that better than a match where the goals simply will not stop. Across three seasons, the league has averaged **7.3 goals per game** — but some fixtures have gone far beyond that. Here are the highest-scoring matches in Baller League UK history.

## 1. NDL FC 9-8 Gold Devils FC — 17 goals (Season 1, GW11)

The single most chaotic match ever played in the league. **NDL FC** and **Gold Devils** traded blows in a **17-goal** epic, with NDL edging it **9-8**. It remains the benchmark for just how wild a Baller League game can get. [Full report](/match/s1-ndl-fc-vs-gold-devils-fc-gw11).

## 2. M7 9-5 Rukkas FC — 14 goals (Season 1, GW11)

A week later in Season 1, **M7** put nine past **Rukkas FC** in a **14-goal** rout — one of the great individual team scoring displays. [Full report](/match/s1-m7-fc-vs-rukkas-fc-gw11).

## 3. M7 7-7 Yanited — 14 goals (Season 2, GW4)

A **14-goal thriller** that ended level. **M7** and **Yanited** (now **RTG FC**) shared the spoils in a 7-7 draw that had everything. [Full report](/match/s2-m7-fc-vs-yanited-gw4).

## 4. Yanited 8-6 N5 FC — 14 goals (Season 3, GW4)

Another 14-goal classic, this time in Season 3, as **Yanited** outscored **N5** 8-6. [Full report](/match/s3-yanited-vs-n5-fc-gw4).

## 5. Deportrio 8-6 Prime FC — 14 goals (Season 3, GW8)

Two heavyweight attacks collided and produced 14 goals, with **Deportrio** beating eventual champions **Prime FC** 8-6. [Full report](/match/s3-deportrio-vs-prime-fc-gw8).

## 6. Clutch FC 8-6 Community FC — 14 goals (Season 3, GW11)

**Clutch** closed out the Season 3 regular season with a 14-goal win over **Community FC**. [Full report](/match/s3-clutch-fc-vs-community-fc-gw11).

## 7. VZN FC 7-6 N5 FC — 13 goals (Season 1, GW3)

A **13-goal** cracker from the league's first season, decided by a single goal. [Full report](/match/s1-vzn-fc-vs-n5-fc-gw3).

## 8. Clutch FC 4-9 Gold Devils FC — 13 goals (Season 2, GW8)

**Gold Devils** ran riot in a **13-goal** encounter, putting nine past Clutch. [Full report](/match/s2-clutch-fc-vs-gold-devils-fc-gw8).

## Why the Baller League produces so many goals

Three factors combine to make these scorelines possible:

1. **Small-sided football.** With six players a side, there is more space and more chances.
2. **Short matches, fast tempo.** Two 15-minute halves mean teams push for goals from the first whistle.
3. **The Game Changers.** Twice per match, the rules tilt towards attack — and the goals follow. The **1-on-1** and **3Play** windows are especially prolific.
