## Mauro Vilhete: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Mauro Vilhete** is a midfielder for **Prime FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Barnet and São Martinho player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Mauro Vilhete is a versatile wide player best known for an outstanding career at **Barnet**, where he made over **300 appearances** across more than a decade. Equally comfortable at full-back or on the wing, he is renowned for his energy, direct running and consistency.

His durability and experience make him a reliable, hard-working presence in the Prime FC squad.

## Career

Mauro Vilhete built his professional career at Barnet and São Martinho. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.
