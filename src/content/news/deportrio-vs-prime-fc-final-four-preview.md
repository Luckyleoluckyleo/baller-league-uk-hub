## Deportrio vs Prime FC — Semi-Final 2

The second semi-final is a clash of the league's two most prolific attacks. **Deportrio** (50 goals) and **Prime FC** (55 goals — the league's best attack) meet for a second time this season after their GW8 classic where Deportrio edged it 8-6.

### The Teams

**Deportrio** (2nd — 7W 2D 2L — 50 GF / 37 GA / +13 GD)
Micah Richards' side finished second on 23 points, matching NDL FC's record but trailing on goal difference. **Jay Emmanuel-Thomas** is the league's joint-top scorer with 18 goals and is Deportrio's talisman. **Sahid Kamara** adds 9 goals from midfield, making Deportrio dangerous from all areas.

**Prime FC** (3rd — 6W 2D 3L — 55 GF / 30 GA / +25 GD)
KSI's team scored a league-high 55 goals and hold the best goal difference at +25. **Francis Gerard Mampolo** netted 17 goals, while **Bráulio Valera Pinto Maieco** added 9. Prime FC are capable of blowing any team away — they scored 7+ goals four times this season including a 7-1 demolition of Wembley Rangers AFC.

### Season 3 Head-to-Head

These teams produced the match of the season in GW8 — Deportrio won 8-6 in a 14-goal thriller. Game Changers: 3Play (3 GC goals) and The Line (4 GC goals).

### Key Matchup

It's attack versus attack. Prime FC's +25 goal difference is superior, but Deportrio beat them when it mattered. JET (18 goals) vs Mampolo (17 goals) is a battle of the league's two best strikers.

### What's at Stake

The winner faces the victor of NDL FC vs SDS FC in the Final Four final. With both teams boasting elite firepower, expect goals.

**Kick-off:** 19:00, Monday 25 May 2026 — The O2, London. Live on Sky Sports, YouTube & Twitch.
