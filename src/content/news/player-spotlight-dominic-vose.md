## Dominic Vose: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Dominic Vose** is a midfielder for **RTG FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former West Ham United, Wrexham, Barnet and Torquay United player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Dominic Vose is a creative attacking midfielder who came through the **West Ham United** academy. A technically gifted player with an eye for a pass and a goal, he went on to represent the likes of **Wrexham**, **Barnet** and **Torquay United**.

Vose brings flair and invention to RTG FC — the kind of player who can unlock a defence in the tight spaces the 6v6 format creates.

## Career

Dominic Vose built his professional career at West Ham United, Wrexham, Barnet and Torquay United. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.
