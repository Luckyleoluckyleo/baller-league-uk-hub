## RTG FC: Season 4 Preview

Danny Aarons' RTG FC — the rebranded Yanited — chase silverware in Season 4. Here is the full lowdown on RTG FC ahead of Baller League UK Season 4 — the manager, the squad, the opening fixture and what to expect.

RTG FC are the Season 4 rebrand of **Yanited**, now led by the YouTube and football-content star **Danny Aarons**. Aarons built a huge following through his football videos, challenges and Misfits boxing appearances, and he brings that same infectious energy to the Baller League touchline. The "Road to Glory" name says it all — this is a team with a point to prove.

The squad blends clinical finishers with relentless, hard-working players who thrive in the chaos that Game Changers create. RTG FC play fast, direct football and never shy away from a physical battle. With Aarons' competitive streak driving them on, they are one of the most intriguing stories of the new season — a familiar franchise with a brand-new identity and everything to play for.
