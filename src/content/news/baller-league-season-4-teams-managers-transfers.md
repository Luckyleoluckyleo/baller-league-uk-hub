## Building towards Season 4

Baller League UK returns on **Monday 12 October 2026** at the new **Baller League Arena** in Dagenham — and the league has been busy reshaping its line-up over the summer. Here's what we know so far.

## Teams and managers

Eleven of the twelve teams look set to return, led by familiar faces: **KSI** (Prime), **Niko Omilana** (NDL), **Micah Richards** and **Daniel Sturridge** (Deportrio), **Chloe Kelly** (Clutch), **Mark Goldbridge** (Gold Devils), **Jens Lehmann** (N5), **Idris Elba** (Rukkas), **Sharky** (SDS), **TBJZL / Tobi Brown** (VZN).

Two notable shifts:
- **Wembley Rangers** are now listed with **both Ian Wright and Alan Shearer** on the touchline — a genuine Premier League-legend double act.
- **Angry Ginge's Yanited do not appear** in the Season 4 line-up, and a **12th team is listed as TBD**. Given the league's talk of **Premier League clubs fielding sides**, that vacant spot could be one to watch.

*These details come from publicly edited sources (Wikipedia, 25 September 2026) and aren't all officially confirmed — we'll verify against ballerleague.uk as it updates.*

## The big signing: Ben Mee

The headline arrival so far is **Ben Mee**, the former **Burnley captain**, who has come out of retirement to join **Deportrio**. The 37-year-old links up with ex-Manchester City and England defender **Joleon Lescott** in a seriously experienced back line.

## Rumours

- **Gymskin** — the viral TikTok/Kick star who appeared at Baller League in 2026 — is widely linked with a **managerial role**. Unconfirmed.

## Off the pitch

Season 4 will be **online-only** after the league's **Sky Sports deal ended**. Expect live coverage across YouTube, Twitch and Kick.

## What's next

The full **fixtures and squads** are expected to be confirmed imminently. Season 4 runs every Monday from **12 October to 21 December 2026**, with the **Final Four** deciding the champion.
