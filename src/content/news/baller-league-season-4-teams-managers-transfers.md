## The line-up is locked in

Baller League UK returns on **Monday 12 October 2026** at the **Baller League Arena** in Dagenham, and the league has confirmed its full **12-team line-up** for Season 4. It is a season of change: two famous franchises have been rebranded, a brand-new club has joined the party, and a fresh wave of recognisable names has arrived on the touchline and in the squads.

This is your one-stop guide to who is in, who is out, who is managing whom, and the signings that have set tongues wagging ahead of opening night.

## The 12 teams

The Season 4 line-up is: **Clutch**, **Deportrio**, **Gold Devils**, **N5**, **NDL**, **P17 FC**, **Prime**, **RTG FC**, **SDS**, **Toma**, **VZN** and **Wembley Rangers**.

Two of those names are rebrands of clubs that have featured since the league began, and one is entirely new:

- **RTG FC** — formerly known as **Yanited**. Now managed by **Danny Aarons**, the popular football-content creator.
- **P17 FC** — formerly **Rukkas FC**. Now managed by **Alex Iwobi**, the Nigeria international and Premier League winger.
- **Toma** — a brand-new franchise for Season 4, managed by the one and only **Jay-Jay Okocha**.

You can explore every side on our [teams hub](/teams), where each profile carries the club's colours, squad and season record.

## The signings to watch

The headline arrival of the summer was **Ben Mee** — the former **Burnley captain** who came out of retirement to join **Deportrio**. The 37-year-old defender links up with ex-Manchester City and England centre-back **Joleon Lescott** in one of the most experienced back lines in the competition.

Deportrio's squad is stacked with former professionals, including striker **Jay Emmanuel-Thomas**, forward **Marvin Sordell** and frontman **Martyn Waghorn**. Elsewhere, **P17 FC** boast former Republic of Ireland defender **Ciaran Clark**, while **VZN** have international defender **Terell Thomas** and **RTG FC** can call on the combative **Calum Butcher**.

## A new era on and off the pitch

There is more to Season 4 than new names. Matches are set to stream **online-only** after the league's deal with traditional broadcasters came to an end, with coverage continuing across the league's own channels.

On the pitch, the format that has made the league a hit remains: **6v6**, two **15-minute halves**, two **wildcards** per team per matchday, and the **Game Changers** that rewrite the rules at the 12th and 27th minutes. The regular season runs for **11 Monday-night matchdays**, with the top four reaching the **Final Four** to decide the champion.
