## Junior Morias: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Junior Morias** is a striker for **P17 FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Peterborough United, Dagenham & Redbridge and Boreham Wood player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Junior Morias is a hard-running English striker who has built a career in the Football League and National League. A powerful, direct forward with a sharp eye for goal, he turned out for **Peterborough United** and **Dagenham & Redbridge** before continuing his career in the non-league game.

His willingness to press, hold the ball up and shoot on sight makes him a natural fit for P17 FC's attacking setup — and for a format where the Game Changer windows reward forwards who are always ready to pull the trigger.

## Career

Junior Morias built his professional career at Peterborough United, Dagenham & Redbridge and Boreham Wood. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.
