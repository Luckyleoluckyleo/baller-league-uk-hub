## Timmy Abraham: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Timmy Abraham** is a striker for **Prime FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Fulham, Cheltenham Town and Bristol Rovers player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Timmy Abraham is a striker who came through the **Fulham** academy, following in the footsteps of his older brother, the England international **Tammy Abraham**. A strong, mobile forward, he gained senior experience on loan in the Football League before continuing his career in the lower divisions.

At Prime FC he adds another genuine goalscoring option to a squad already packed with attacking talent.

## Career

Timmy Abraham built his professional career at Fulham, Cheltenham Town and Bristol Rovers. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.
