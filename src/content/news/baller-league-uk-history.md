## A young league making rapid strides

Baller League UK is one of the youngest competitions in British football, yet it has already packed in four seasons, three different champions and a high-profile move away from traditional television. This is the story of the league so far.

## What is Baller League UK?

Baller League is a **6-a-side** football competition built for entertainment. Teams play two **15-minute halves**, with **two wildcards** per side and the signature **Game Changers** — six rule changes activated at the 12th and 27th minutes of every match. The UK edition launched in **2025**, bringing the format (already popular in Germany) to an English audience through major broadcast coverage before moving online-only for Season 4.

## Season 1 (2025)

The league's inaugural UK season introduced the 12-team, 11-matchday format and crowned its first champion. **SDS FC** went all the way, winning the Final **4-3 against Prime FC** after a playoff run that had knocked out Deportrio (who had topped the regular season).

- **Regular-season leaders:** Deportrio
- **Champions:** SDS FC

## Season 2 (2025–26)

Season 2 saw a new regular-season pace-setter in **M7**, but it was **Wembley Rangers AFC** who lifted the title. They beat SDS FC **4-3 in the Final**, denying SDS back-to-back championships.

- **Regular-season leaders:** M7
- **Champions:** Wembley Rangers AFC

## Season 3 (2026)

Season 3 delivered one of the tightest campaigns yet. **NDL** topped the table on **26 points**, with **Deportrio** (23), **Prime FC** (21) and **SDS** (20) completing the Final Four. In the playoffs, Prime — the third seed — beat Deportrio **5-3** in their semi-final, then dismantled NDL **5-2** in the Final at a sold-out **O2 Arena** to be crowned champions. Read the [full Season 3 review](/news/baller-league-season-3-review-prime-fc-champions).

- **Regular-season leaders:** NDL
- **Champions:** Prime FC

## Season 4 (2026) — a new era

Season 4 arrives with major changes. The league moved to an **online-only** broadcast model after its **Sky Sports** deal ended, and the line-up was reshaped:

- **Yanited → RTG FC**, now managed by **Danny Aarons**
- **Rukkas FC → P17 FC**, now managed by **Alex Iwobi**
- **Toma** joined as a brand-new franchise under **Jay-Jay Okocha**

Read the [full Season 4 line-up](/news/baller-league-season-4-teams-managers-transfers). Season 4 runs every Monday from **12 October to 21 December 2026**, with the top four again reaching the Final Four.
