## Nya Kirby: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Nya Kirby** is a midfielder for **SDS FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Crystal Palace, Blackpool and Tranmere Rovers player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Nya Kirby is a technically gifted central midfielder who came through the **Crystal Palace** academy. He was part of the **England** squad that won the **U-17 World Cup in 2017**, one of the standout achievements of English youth football.

A composed passer with a good engine, Kirby is the kind of midfielder who makes a team tick — and his ability to keep the ball under pressure is invaluable in the shot-clock Game Changer formats.

## Career

Nya Kirby built his professional career at Crystal Palace, Blackpool and Tranmere Rovers. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.

## Honours & achievements

- FIFA U-17 World Cup winner with England (2017)
