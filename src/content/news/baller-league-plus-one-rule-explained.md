**Plus One restarts the game as a 1v1, goalkeepers included, and adds one outfield player to each side for every goal scored**, rebuilding towards full 5v5 as the window goes on.

It is the only Game Changer where the shape of the match changes with the scoreline — and the one that starts at its most open.

## When Plus One activates

Plus One is a **first-half Game Changer**, announced at the **12th minute**. Across Seasons 1–3 it has been activated **68 times** — 16.7% of all Game Changer activations.

## Why the start of the window matters most

Because the restart is 1v1, the first few seconds are the most dangerous of the entire activation. Two players, an open pitch, and a shot clock — it is as close to pure one-on-one football as the league gets.

Every goal then adds a player back to each side. The window tightens as it fills up: by the time both teams are close to full numbers, the space that made the opening so dangerous has gone. That is why Plus One rarely produces the goal avalanches seen under 3Play, even though it starts in a more extreme format.

One quirk worth knowing: there are no time penalties during Plus One. A foul gives away a penalty instead.

## How teams win Plus One

- **Win the first duel.** The opening 1v1 is the best chance either side will get in the window.
- **Score early, then settle.** Each goal brings both teams closer to full numbers, so the longer the window stays open, the more chances there are.
- **Do not force it late.** Once the sides are back to near-full strength, the space is gone and turnovers are expensive.
