## Josh Parker: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Josh Parker** is a striker for **NDL FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Reading, Oxford United and AFC Wimbledon player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Josh Parker is a powerful, experienced striker who came through at **Reading** and went on to play across the Football League and in several countries abroad. A regular for the **Antigua and Barbuda** national team, he combines physical presence with a good eye for goal.

Parker's hold-up play and finishing give NDL an experienced focal point in the final third.

## Career

Josh Parker built his professional career at Reading, Oxford United and AFC Wimbledon. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.

## Honours & achievements

- Antigua and Barbuda international
