**The Onside Gamechanger suspends the offside rule entirely for the duration of the window.** Attackers can position themselves wherever they like, with no last defender to beat and no line to hold.

That only makes sense if you know the starting point: Baller League UK **does** have offside the rest of the time.

## Does Baller League UK have an offside rule?

Yes. Offside is in play during normal 6v6, using a dedicated offside line between the halfway line and the penalty area. Two exceptions matter:

- It is **suspended completely** while the Onside Gamechanger is active.
- It **does not apply from a kick-in**, which is how the league restarts play instead of throw-ins.

So the answer to "does Baller League have offside" is yes — but the rule you see for three minutes at a time is not the rule you see for the other 27.

## When Onside activates

Onside is a **first-half Gamechanger**, announced at the **12th minute** and live for a short window. Across Seasons 1–3 it has been activated **59 times** — 14.5% of all Gamechanger activations.

## Why removing offside doesn't flood the scoreline

You would expect no offside to mean a rush of goals. The data says otherwise: **25 of 59 Onside windows produced no goals at all (42%)**, at just **0.86 goals per activation** — the second-lowest of the six rules.

The likely reason is that removing the offside line does not create space by itself. Defences simply drop deeper and defend the box rather than the line, so the game compresses rather than stretches. The teams that profit are the ones that attack the space immediately, before the block settles.

## How teams win Onside

- **Attack immediately.** The value is in the first few seconds, before the defence reorganises without a line.
- **Press the ball, not the space.** With no offside trap available, stopping the pass at source is the only reliable defence.
- **Keep a runner high.** A forward who never tracks back is far more valuable during this window than in normal 6v6.
