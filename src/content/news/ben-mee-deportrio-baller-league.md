## A Premier League skipper signs on

**Ben Mee** has become the latest former Premier League star to join Baller League UK. The **ex-Burnley captain**, 37, has signed for **Deportrio** — the team managed by **Micah Richards** and **Daniel Sturridge** — just months after announcing his retirement from professional football in May.

The signing was announced on Baller League's official Instagram and reported by **The Sun**.

## A serious defensive addition

Mee spent a decade at **Burnley**, captaining the Clarets through some of the most successful years in the club's modern history, and finished his career with spells at **Brentford** and **Sheffield United**. Since retiring he's moved into punditry — but the pull of the six-a-side game has brought him back onto the pitch.

He joins a Deportrio squad that already includes ex-Manchester City and England defender **Joleon Lescott**, giving the side a genuinely formidable back line for the fast-paced 6v6 format.
