## Watching the Baller League in Season 4

Baller League UK Season 4 has moved to a fully **online-only broadcast model** after the league's Sky Sports deal came to an end. The good news for fans is simple: you no longer need a TV subscription to watch — every matchday is streamed directly by the league across its digital channels.

Here is everything you need to know to follow the new season.

## When does it take place?

Season 4 runs every **Monday night** from **12 October to 21 December 2026**, with matches staged at the **Baller League Arena** in Dagenham, London. Each matchday features **six fixtures**, kicking off from around **17:30** and running until roughly **22:15**.

## Where to watch online

Live coverage and clips are published across the league's own channels:

- **YouTube** — live streams, highlights and full-match replays on the official Baller League UK channel.
- **Twitch** — free live streams on matchdays.
- **Kick** — live coverage and community streams.
- **Social clips** — Instagram, TikTok and X carry goals and moments throughout the night.

Because the league streams directly, the action is **free to watch** from anywhere with an internet connection. Check our dedicated [Where to Watch page](/watch) for the latest links and any confirmed changes.

## How the matchday works

Each Monday, all **12 teams** are involved across **six matches**. Every game is **6v6**, split into two **15-minute halves**, with two **wildcards** per team and the **Game Changers** triggering at the 12th and 27th minutes. That means a full evening of fast, high-scoring football — usually with a shock or two.
