## Sky Sports deal ends

Baller League UK will no longer be shown on **Sky Sports**, with the league shifting to a fully **online-only broadcast model** ahead of Season 4. The change was first reported by the **BBC** in August 2026 and followed up by **Net Influencer**.

For the league's first seasons, Sky Sports carried live coverage and highlights, helping bring Baller League UK to a mainstream television audience. From Season 4, fans will instead follow the action through the league's digital channels.

## What it means for fans

If you watched Baller League UK on Sky, the good news is that the matches themselves aren't going anywhere — they'll be streamed directly by the league. Expect live coverage and clips across **YouTube, Twitch and Kick**, where a lot of the league's audience already follows the action.

We'll keep our [Where to Watch](/watch) page updated with the confirmed Season 4 broadcast details as they're announced.

## Manager reaction

The change hasn't passed without comment. **Angry Ginge**, who manages Yanited, was reported by **The Sun** to have reacted strongly to being "dropped" from Sky Sports, in a characteristically unfiltered rant.
