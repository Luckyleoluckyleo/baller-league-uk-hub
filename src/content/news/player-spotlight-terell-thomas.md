## Terell Thomas: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Terell Thomas** is a defender for **VZN FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Arsenal (youth), Charlton Athletic, Wigan Athletic and AFC Wimbledon player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Terell Thomas is an athletic, ball-playing defender who came through the Arsenal academy before establishing himself in the English Football League with Charlton Athletic, Wigan Athletic and AFC Wimbledon. He also represents Antigua and Barbuda at international level.

Comfortable in possession and quick across the ground, Thomas is well suited to the pace of the 6v6 game, where defenders must read danger early and spring attacks. He adds athleticism and composure to VZN's back line.

## Career

Terell Thomas built his professional career at Arsenal (youth), Charlton Athletic, Wigan Athletic and AFC Wimbledon. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.
