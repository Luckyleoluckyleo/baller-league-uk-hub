## Toma vs Gold Devils FC — Gameweek 1 Preview

The new Baller League UK season gets under way as **Toma** take on **Gold Devils FC** in Gameweek 1 on **12 October 2026**, with kick-off at **21:20**. It is the first competitive outing of Season 4 for both clubs, and the opening night at the Baller League Arena in Dagenham promises the usual mix of fast 6v6 football, celebrity dugouts and the game-changing rule twists that make the league so watchable.

Season 4 marks a fresh start. Yanited have been rebranded as **RTG FC**, Rukkas FC are now **P17 FC**, and **Toma** join the league as a brand-new franchise — but the format remains the same: 12 teams, 11 Monday-night matchdays and a Final Four deciding the champion.
