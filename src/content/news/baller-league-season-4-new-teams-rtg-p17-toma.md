## A summer of change

Every new season brings new faces, but Season 4 of Baller League UK has gone further than most. Two of the league's longest-standing franchises have been rebranded, and a brand-new club has joined the competition. If you are coming back to the Baller League after a break, here is everything you need to know about the three new names on the team sheet.

## RTG FC — Yanited reborn

**RTG FC** is the new identity of a club that fans will remember as **Yanited**. The franchise has a new look, a new name and a new manager: **Danny Aarons**, the football-content creator whose videos, challenges and personality have built him a huge following online.

The "Road to Glory" branding says it all. This is a team with a point to prove — a familiar badge with a fresh start and a manager who brings relentless competitiveness and banter in equal measure. You can read the full [RTG FC team profile](/teams/yanited) and [Danny Aarons' manager profile](/managers/danny-aarons) on the hub.

## P17 FC — Alex Iwobi takes the reins

**P17 FC** is the rebranded **Rukkas FC**, and it arrives with genuine top-flight pedigree in the dugout. **Alex Iwobi** — the Nigeria international and Premier League winger who came through the Arsenal academy before spells at Everton and Fulham — is the new manager.

Iwobi's flair, close control and football intelligence make him a natural fit for the fast, technical 6v6 format. Expect P17 to play expansive, attack-minded football with an emphasis on quick combinations — the kind of style that thrives when the Game Changers open the game up. Explore the [P17 FC team page](/teams/rukkas-fc) and [Alex Iwobi's profile](/managers/alex-iwobi).

## Toma — the new franchise with a legend in charge

**Toma** are the newest club in the league, and they could not have picked a more fitting figure to lead them. **Jay-Jay Okocha** — the former Nigeria captain and Bolton Wanderers icon, famous for his outrageous skill and no-look passes — takes charge of the new franchise.

Okocha spent a career making defenders look foolish, and his philosophy of joyful, expressive football is a perfect match for the Baller League's showstopping format. Toma are the great unknown of Season 4: a brand-new team with a legendary name in the dugout and flair baked into everything they do. Meet the [Toma team](/teams/toma) and read [Jay-Jay Okocha's story](/managers/jay-jay-okocha).

## Why the changes matter

Rebrands and new franchises are part of what keeps the Baller League fresh. A new identity can reset a club's momentum, while a marquee manager transforms the way a team is watched and supported. For neutrals, it also means three genuinely new stories to follow when the season kicks off on **12 October 2026**.
