## Prime FC: champions of Season 3

Baller League UK Season 3 ended with **Prime FC** lifting the trophy in front of a sold-out crowd at **The O2 Arena** — a dramatic conclusion to a campaign that went right to the wire. Managed by **KSI**, Prime saved their best football for the moments that mattered most, peaking in the playoffs to be crowned champions.

Here is how their title-winning season unfolded.

## The regular season

The 11-gameweek regular season produced a genuinely tight race. **NDL** topped the table with **26 points**, edging **Deportrio** (23) into second, while **Prime** finished third on **21 points** — despite possessing the best goal difference in the league at **+25**, a hint of the attacking firepower that would prove decisive later.

**SDS** claimed the fourth and final playoff spot on **20 points**, pipping **Wembley Rangers** (18) and **Clutch** (17). At the other end, **Community Team** endured a difficult campaign, finishing bottom.

The top-four finish earned Prime a **Final Four** place — but as the third seed, they entered the playoffs as the outsiders.

## The Final Four

The season's climax was staged at **The O2 Arena**, with two semi-finals and the Final played back-to-back.

**Semi-final: Deportrio 3-5 Prime FC.** In a high-scoring opener, Prime overturned higher-seeded Deportrio in a thriller, their attacking quality telling in a five-goal display that sent them into the Final.

**Semi-final: NDL 3-1 SDS.** The league leaders, NDL, saw off SDS to set up a showpiece against Prime — a meeting of the regular season's best side and its most in-form attack.

**Final: NDL 2-5 Prime FC.** And then came the crowning moment. Prime produced their most complete performance of the season, beating NDL **5-2** to be crowned **Baller League UK Season 3 champions**. Against the team that had led the table from the front, KSI's side delivered when it counted — proof that in the Baller League, peaking at the right time is everything.

## The story of the season

Prime's triumph captured what makes the Baller League so compelling: a regular-season leader is not guaranteed the title, and a team that finishes third can go all the way. Their league-best goal difference underlined the attacking identity that ultimately carried them over the line in the knockout format.

For NDL, it was a painful near-miss after a superb regular season. For the neutrals, it was another reminder that the **Game Changers** and the high-tempo 6v6 format can swing a match — and a season — in an instant.
