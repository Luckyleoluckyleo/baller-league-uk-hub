## Rowan Liburd: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Rowan Liburd** is a striker for **RTG FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Reading, Wycombe Wanderers and Boreham Wood player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Rowan Liburd is a strong, athletic striker who came through at **Reading** before moving on to **Wycombe Wanderers** and a career in the Football League and National League. A genuine target man, he combines physical presence with a decent turn of pace.

At RTG FC he gives the side a focal point in attack — the kind of forward who can hold the ball up under pressure and finish when the chance arrives.

## Career

Rowan Liburd built his professional career at Reading, Wycombe Wanderers and Boreham Wood. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.
