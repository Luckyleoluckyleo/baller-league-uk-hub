## The new season starts here

Baller League UK **Season 4** begins on **Monday 12 October 2026** at the **Baller League Arena** in Dagenham, with **six matches** on opening night and every one of the league's 12 teams in action. Kick-off times run from **17:30** through to **22:15**, so it is a full evening of 6v6 football to launch the new campaign.

Here is your fixture-by-fixture guide to Gameweek 1.

## 17:30 — Deportrio vs Wembley Rangers

The season opens with **Deportrio**, managed by **Micah Richards**, taking on **Wembley Rangers**. Deportrio have assembled one of the most experienced squads in the league — led by the headline signing **Ben Mee** alongside **Joleon Lescott** — while Wembley Rangers begin the new campaign with their manager yet to be announced. Read the [Deportrio team preview](/news/season-4-team-preview-deportrio) and [Wembley Rangers preview](/news/season-4-team-preview-wembley-rangers-afc).

## 18:30 — VZN FC vs RTG FC

**Tobi Brown's VZN** meet the rebranded **RTG FC**, now managed by **Danny Aarons**. It is a first competitive outing for the new RTG identity, and a chance for Aarons to make an early statement. See the [VZN preview](/news/season-4-team-preview-vzn-fc) and [RTG FC preview](/news/season-4-team-preview-yanited).

## 19:30 — SDS FC vs N5 FC

**Sharky's SDS** face **Jens Lehmann's N5** in a meeting of two well-established sides. Lehmann — the former Arsenal and Germany goalkeeper — remains one of the most decorated managers in the league. Read the [SDS preview](/news/season-4-team-preview-sds-fc) and [N5 preview](/news/season-4-team-preview-n5-fc).

## 20:25 — NDL FC vs P17 FC

**Niko Omilana's NDL** take on **Alex Iwobi's P17 FC**, the rebranded Rukkas side. Iwobi's arrival gives P17 genuine Premier League pedigree in the dugout, and his side will look to start fast. See the [NDL preview](/news/season-4-team-preview-ndl-fc) and [P17 FC preview](/news/season-4-team-preview-rukkas-fc).

## 21:20 — Toma vs Gold Devils

The league's newest franchise, **Toma**, make their debut against **Gold Devils**, co-managed by **Mark Goldbridge** and **Spencer Owen**. All eyes will be on **Jay-Jay Okocha's** new side as they step into competitive action for the first time. Read the [Toma preview](/news/season-4-team-preview-toma) and [Gold Devils preview](/news/season-4-team-preview-gold-devils-fc).

## 22:15 — Prime FC vs Clutch FC

Opening night closes with a blockbuster: **KSI's Prime**, the reigning champions, against **Chloe Kelly's Clutch**. Prime lifted the Season 3 title and will want to lay down an early marker. See the [Prime preview](/news/season-4-team-preview-prime-fc) and [Clutch preview](/news/season-4-team-preview-clutch-fc).

## How the season works

Every team plays **11 Monday-night matchdays** in the regular season, followed by the **Final Four** playoffs. Each season consists of **6v6** football across two **15-minute halves**, with two **wildcards** per team per matchday and the famous **Game Changers** triggering at the 12th and 27th minutes.

New to the league? Our [Game Changers explained](/news/baller-league-game-changers-explained) guide walks through every rule twist.
