## Toma: Season 4 Preview

New for Season 4 — Jay-Jay Okocha's Toma bring legendary flair to the Baller League. Here is the full lowdown on Toma ahead of Baller League UK Season 4 — the manager, the squad, the opening fixture and what to expect.

Toma are one of the newest franchises in Baller League UK, arriving for Season 4 under the guidance of one of football's greatest entertainers: **Jay-Jay Okocha**. The former Nigeria captain and Premier League icon — who lit up Bolton and the world stage with his outrageous skill — is exactly the kind of figure the 6v6 format was made for.

Okocha's philosophy is simple: football should be fun. Expect Toma to play with freedom, trickery and no shortage of showmanship, encouraging players to express themselves in the tight, high-tempo Game Changer moments. Backed by a manager who spent a career making defenders look foolish, Toma are the league's great unknown — a brand-new team with a legendary name in the dugout and flair baked into everything they do.
