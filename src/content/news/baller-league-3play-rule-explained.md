**3Play turns a Baller League UK match into 3-a-side, goalkeeper included, with a 30-second shot clock per possession.** Lose the ball, or fail to get a shot away before the clock runs out, and possession is handed to the opposition.

It is the most frequently activated Game Changer in the league — and one of the most productive.

## When 3Play activates

3Play is a **first-half Game Changer**, announced at the **12th minute** and live for a short window before normal 6v6 rules resume. It never appears in the second half; that window belongs to the other three rules.

Across Seasons 1–3 it has been activated **78 times** — 19.2% of all Game Changer activations, more than any other rule in the league.

## Why 3Play scores so heavily

The format does the work. Three players a side means the pitch is far too big to defend comfortably, so a single beaten defender creates a shooting chance rather than a scramble. Add the 30-second shot clock and possession becomes expensive: holding the ball is not an option, so teams shoot earlier and take risks they would avoid at 6v6.

The result is a window where the game speeds up dramatically in both directions.

## How teams win 3Play

- **Shoot first time.** With a 30-second clock, an extra touch is often the difference between a shot and a turnover.
- **Press the ball, not the space.** In 3v3 there is no spare defender to cover a beaten man.
- **Keep one player high.** A permanent outlet means every turnover can become an immediate counter.
