## Mustapha Carayol: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Mustapha Carayol** is a midfielder for **N5 FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Middlesbrough, Bristol City, Huddersfield Town and Ipswich Town player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Mustapha Carayol is a pacy, skilful winger who played at the top of the Championship for **Middlesbrough**, **Bristol City**, **Huddersfield Town** and **Ipswich Town**. A Gambia international, he is known for his direct running, trickery in one-v-one situations and a fierce shot.

Carayol's dribbling makes him a nightmare in the one-on-one Game Changer, and his ability to beat a man from nothing is exactly what the Baller League format rewards.

## Career

Mustapha Carayol built his professional career at Middlesbrough, Bristol City, Huddersfield Town and Ipswich Town. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.
