**The 1-on-1 Game Changer replaces team shape with isolated duels.** Each player stays in their own half with a 15-second shot clock per possession, so defending becomes an individual job and there is nowhere to hide.

It is the most explosive rule in the league, with a higher goals-per-activation rate than any other Game Changer.

## When 1-on-1 activates

1-on-1 is a **second-half Game Changer**, announced at the **27th minute**. That timing is part of its impact: it lands when matches are already stretched and legs are tired.

Across Seasons 1–3 it has been activated **72 times** — 17.7% of all activations.

## Why it produces the most goals

Every other rule keeps some version of a team structure. 1-on-1 removes it. With players restricted to their own half and a 15-second clock, a match becomes a series of individual contests: beat your man and you shoot, lose the duel and the attack dies.

That means the outcome of the window usually comes down to who has the better one-on-one defenders and finishers on the pitch at that moment — and why it is the rule most likely to be decided by a single player.

## 1-on-1 by the numbers

Figures cover Seasons 1–3 (406 Game Changer activations in total).

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Activations | **72** |
| Goals scored during 1-on-1 | **202** |
| Goals per activation | **2.81** |
| Share of all activations | **17.7%** |
| Windows with no goals | 6 (8%) |

At **2.81 goals per activation** it edges 3Play (2.58) as the most productive of the six rules — despite being activated less often.

## How teams win 1-on-1

- **Keep your best duel-winner on the pitch.** This is the rule where individual quality matters most.
- **Defend the first touch.** With a 15-second clock, stopping the initial turn is usually enough.
- **Shoot early.** Waiting for a better angle in a duel is how the clock runs out.
