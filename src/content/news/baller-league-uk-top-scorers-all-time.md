## The league's most prolific finishers

Scoring in the Baller League is not easy, even in a format averaging **7.3 goals a game**. Doing it consistently, season after season, is the mark of a genuinely elite forward. Here are the **top 20 goalscorers in Baller League UK history**, based on every recorded goal across the competition's first three seasons.

## The top 20

1. **[Francis Mampolo](/players/francis-gerard-mampolo)** — **45 goals.** The league's all-time leading scorer and the benchmark for every striker in the competition.
2. **[Bilal Sayoud](/players/bilal-sayoud)** — **42 goals.** A relentless finisher who has been near the top of the charts from the start.
3. **[Kazaiah Sterling](/players/kazaiah-sterling)** — **42 goals.** The former Tottenham forward is one of the league's most reliable match-winners.
4. **[Tyler Winters](/players/tyler-winters)** — **37 goals.** A prolific striker who has tormented defences across multiple seasons.
5. **[Edy Maieco](/players/braulio-valera-pinto-maieco)** — **37 goals.** A clinical presence in the final third.
6. **[Domingos Pires](/players/domingos-pires)** — **31 goals.** A consistent source of goals in the league's early seasons.
7. **Bryan Ly** — **30 goals.** One of the most under-rated forwards in the competition.
8. **[Amin Belaid](/players/amin-belaid)** — **30 goals.** A fine finisher and a key attacking figure at NDL.
9. **[Stefan Ilic](/players/stefan-ilic)** — **30 goals.** A dangerous forward with an eye for goal.
10. **[Alfie Matthews](/players/alfie-matthews)** — **28 goals.** A threat in the Clutch attack.
11. **[Emmanuel Robe](/players/emmanuel-robe)** — **28 goals.** A reliable goalscorer for Deportrio.
12. **[Michael Hill](/players/michael-hill)** — **27 goals.** A productive attacking outlet.
13. **[Sam Fitzgerald](/players/sam-fitzgerald)** — **27 goals.** A creative force who also chips in with goals.
14. **[Kadell Daniel](/players/kadell-daniel)** — **26 goals.** A pacey attacker and a regular scorer.
15. **[Ergys Pepaj](/players/ergys-pepaj)** — **25 goals.** A composed finisher.
16. **[Jay Emmanuel-Thomas](/players/jay-emmanuel-thomas)** — **25 goals.** The ex-Arsenal forward is one of the league's marquee marksmen.
17. **[Richard Afrane-Kesey](/players/richard-afrane-kesey)** — **23 goals.** A prolific option in the Clutch attack.
18. **[Florian Kastrati](/players/florian-kastrati)** — **23 goals.** A steady goalscoring presence.
19. **[Sandro Camara](/players/sandro-camara)** — **22 goals.** A creative attacker who scores and creates.
20. **[Amine Sassi](/players/amine-sassi)** — **21 goals.** An influential midfielder with an eye for goal.

## The race for the record

**Francis Mampolo** leads the way on **45 goals**, but with **Bilal Sayoud** and **Kazaiah Sterling** both on 42, the top of the charts is razor-thin. Even a single prolific season could change the order.

Several elite scorers are also returning for **Season 4**, with the Game Changer windows — including the goal-heavy **1-on-1** and **3Play** activations — giving the league's forwards every chance to climb the list.

## Best of the rest

Just outside the top 20 sit a host of proven finishers ready to break in during Season 4. With new managers and reshaped squads across the league, the scoring charts could look very different by December.
