## Baller League UK Season 4 is live

Baller League UK's fourth season is under way, having kicked off on **Monday 12 October 2026** and running through to **Monday 21 December 2026**. The 2026 campaign has a new home — the **Baller League Arena at Eastbrook Studios in Dagenham, London** — and a new schedule built around **Monday night football**.

This is the season the league has been building towards, with more matches, closer access to the action and a stacked line-up of celebrity managers and former professionals.

| **Regular-season matchdays** | 11 |

| **Teams** | 12 |
| **Matches per matchday** | 6 |
| **Halves** | 15 minutes |
| **Wildcards** | 2 per team, per matchday |
| **Venue** | Baller League Arena, Eastbrook Studios, Yew Tree Avenue, Dagenham, RM10 7FN |

Every Monday night, six live matches kick off between **17:15 and 21:50**. As always, the **Gamechangers** transform the final three minutes of each half, flipping matches on their head with unpredictable rule changes — from **Onside** and **Plus One** to **3Play**, **1-on-1**, **The Line** and **Fairplay**.

New for Season 4, **each team can select two wildcard players per matchday**, meaning squads can be shuffled week to week. Expect former Premier League players, internationals, non-league standouts and some surprise names.

The Game Changers remain the league's signature twist. They arrive at the **12th and 27th minutes** of each match, changing the rules for a short window and often deciding the outcome. For a full explanation of each one, see our [Game Changers explained](/news/baller-league-game-changers-explained) guide and the [live Gamechanger analysis](/gamechangers).

## Tickets and the Baller League Arena

Season 4 is staged at the **Baller League Arena** at Eastbrook Studios in Dagenham. Three ticket types are on sale:

- **Single Matchday** — access to all six matches on one Monday.
- **Baller Bundle** — multiple consecutive matchdays (three or four nights).
- **Baller Pass** — all 11 regular-season matchdays.

The event is family-friendly; under-16s must be accompanied by an adult. For the full venue, travel and ticket guide, see our [Baller League Arena guide](/news/baller-league-arena-tickets-guide) and the [where to watch page](/watch).
