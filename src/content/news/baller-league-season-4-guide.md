## Baller League UK is back for Season 4

Baller League UK returns for a fourth season, starting **Monday 12 October 2026** and running through to **Monday 21 December 2026**. The 2026 campaign has a new home — the **Baller League Arena at Eastbrook Studios in Dagenham, London** — and a new schedule built around **Monday night football**.

This is the season the league has been building towards, with more matches, closer access to the action and a stacked line-up of celebrity managers and former professionals.

## Key dates and format

| | |
|---|---|
| **Season** | 12 October – 21 December 2026 |
| **Matchdays** | Every Monday |
| **Regular-season matchdays** | 11 |
| **Playoffs** | Final Four |
| **Teams** | 12 |
| **Matches per matchday** | 6 |
| **Halves** | 15 minutes |
| **Wildcards** | 2 per team, per matchday |
| **Venue** | Baller League Arena, Eastbrook Studios, Yew Tree Avenue, Dagenham, RM10 7FN |

Every Monday night, six live matches run from **5:00 PM to 11:00 PM**. As always, the **Gamechangers** transform the final three minutes of each half, flipping matches on their head with unpredictable rule changes — from **Onside** and **Plus One** to **3Play**, **1-on-1**, **The Line** and **Fairplay**.

New for Season 4, **each team can select two wildcard players per matchday**, meaning squads can be shuffled week to week. Expect former Premier League players, internationals, non-league standouts and some surprise names.

## The managers

The celebrity manager line-up is led by returning names including **KSI** (Prime), **Niko Omilana** (NDL), **Micah Richards** and **Daniel Sturridge** (Deportrio) and **Chloe Kelly** (Clutch), alongside the rest of the league's influencer and ex-pro contingent. We'll confirm any new faces as the league announces them.

## Tickets

Three ticket types are on sale:

- **Single Matchday** — access to all six matches on one Monday.
- **Baller Bundle** — multiple consecutive matchdays (three or four nights).
- **Baller Pass** — all 11 regular-season matchdays.

The event is family-friendly; under-16s must be accompanied by an adult.
