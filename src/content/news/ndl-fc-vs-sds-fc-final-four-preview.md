## NDL FC vs SDS FC — Semi-Final 1

The opening semi-final of the Baller League UK Season 3 Final Four pits league leaders **NDL FC** against **SDS FC** — a rematch of their GW8 3-3 draw. Niko Omilana's side topped the regular season with 26 points, while Tobi Brown's SDS scraped into fourth place with 20 points, just edging out Wembley Rangers AFC.

### The Teams

**NDL FC** (1st — 7W 2D 2L — 51 GF / 36 GA / +15 GD)
The league's most consistent team. NDL FC lost only twice all season and boast the second-best attack with 51 goals scored. Their midfield engine of **Dara Dada** (8 goals) and **Sam Fitzgerald** (8 goals) has been relentless, while goalkeeper Dillon Barnes provided a solid last line of defence.

**SDS FC** (4th — 5W 4D 2L — 41 GF / 30 GA / +11 GD)
The Season 1 champions return to The O2 with the best defensive record among the Final Four (only 30 conceded). SDS are hard to beat — only two losses all season. **Connor Wood** leads their scoring with 6 goals from defence, with **Camilo Restrepo** and **Joshua Abbott** chipping in 5 each.

### Season 3 Head-to-Head

The teams met once this season in GW8, playing out a 3-3 thriller. SDS FC led twice before NDL FC fought back. Game Changers: PlusOne (2 GC goals) and Fairplay (0 GC goals).

### Key Matchup

NDL FC's high-pressing attack versus SDS FC's organised defence. SDS conceded only 30 goals in 11 games — the best in the Final Four. NDL FC's ability to score from midfield could be the difference.

### What's at Stake

The winner advances to the Final Four final later that evening. The loser's season is over.

**Kick-off:** 18:00, Monday 25 May 2026 — The O2, London. Live on Sky Sports, YouTube & Twitch.
