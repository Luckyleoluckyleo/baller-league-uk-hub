## Kieron Freeman: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Kieron Freeman** is a defender for **N5 FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Sheffield United, Nottingham Forest, Derby County and Walsall player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Kieron Freeman is a right-back with extensive experience in the Championship and League One. A **Nottingham Forest** academy product, he spent the best years of his career at **Sheffield United**, where he chipped in with goals from full-back, and also turned out for **Derby County**.

His overlapping runs and delivery from wide make him well suited to the 6v6 game, where full-backs are effectively wingers.

## Career

Kieron Freeman built his professional career at Sheffield United, Nottingham Forest, Derby County and Walsall. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.
