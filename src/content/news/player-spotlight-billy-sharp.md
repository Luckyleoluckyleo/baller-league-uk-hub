## Billy Sharp: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Billy Sharp** is a striker for **N5 FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Sheffield United, Southampton, Leeds United, Scunthorpe United and Doncaster Rovers player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Billy Sharp is one of the great goalscorers of the modern English Football League. A club legend at **Sheffield United**, he scored goals wherever he went — for **Scunthorpe United**, **Doncaster Rovers**, **Southampton** and **Leeds United** — and won multiple promotions during a career that spanned more than two decades.

Sharp was the Championship's top scorer in 2018–19 and has scored over 200 career league goals. His arrival at N5 FC adds a genuinely elite finisher to the Baller League — a penalty-box predator whose instincts translate perfectly to the 6v6 game.

## Career

Billy Sharp built his professional career at Sheffield United, Southampton, Leeds United, Scunthorpe United and Doncaster Rovers. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.
