## Ciaran Clark: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Ciaran Clark** is a defender for **P17 FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Aston Villa, Newcastle United and Sheffield United player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Ciaran Clark is a composed, left-footed centre-back who brings real Premier League and international pedigree to P17 FC. An Aston Villa academy graduate, he made over 150 appearances for the club before moving to Newcastle United, where he won the Championship in 2016–17 and established himself as a dependable top-flight defender.

A Republic of Ireland international, Clark reads the game well, defends the front foot and is comfortable stepping into midfield with the ball — qualities that translate neatly to the tight, fast 6v6 format. Under Alex Iwobi he adds experience and calm to the P17 back line.

## Career

Ciaran Clark built his professional career at Aston Villa, Newcastle United and Sheffield United. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.

## Honours & achievements

- Republic of Ireland international
- Championship winner with Newcastle United (2016–17)
