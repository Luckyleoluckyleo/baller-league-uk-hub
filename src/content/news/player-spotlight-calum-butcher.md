## Calum Butcher: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Calum Butcher** is a midfielder for **RTG FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Tottenham Hotspur (youth), Dundee United, Millwall and Southend United player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Calum Butcher is a physical, tenacious midfielder who has built a career across the Scottish and English leagues. A Tottenham Hotspur youth product, he made his name in Scotland with Dundee United before returning south to play for Millwall and Southend United.

Butcher's combative style — winning tackles, breaking up play and driving forward — suits the cut-and-thrust of Baller League's 6v6 format, where the Fairplay Game Changer can punish any lapse in discipline. He brings bite and experience to Danny Aarons' RTG FC.

## Career

Calum Butcher built his professional career at Tottenham Hotspur (youth), Dundee United, Millwall and Southend United. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.
