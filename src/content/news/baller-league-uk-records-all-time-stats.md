## The numbers behind the league

Three full seasons of Baller League UK are in the books, and the data paints the picture of a competition that is every bit as high-scoring and chaotic as its reputation suggests. Across **207 matches**, the league has produced **1,510 goals** — an average of **7.3 goals per game**, a figure that underlines just how relentless the 6v6 format is.

With Season 4 on the horizon, here are the records that define the league so far. (All figures cover Seasons 1–3 and update automatically on our [records page](/records).)

## Goal machines: the all-time top scorers

The Baller League's most prolific marksmen have racked up remarkable tallies:

- **[Francis Mampolo](/players/francis-gerard-mampolo)** — **45 goals**, the league's all-time leading scorer.
- **[Bilal Sayoud](/players/bilal-sayoud)** — **42 goals**, among the deadliest finishers in league history.
- **[Kazaiah Sterling](/players/kazaiah-sterling)** — **42 goals**, an elite goalscorer whose record speaks for itself.
- **[Tyler Winters](/players/tyler-winters)** — **37 goals** and counting.
- **[Braulio Valera Pinto Maieco](/players/braulio-valera-pinto-maieco)** — **37 goals**, one of the league's standout attackers.

Breaking 40 goals in this league is a serious achievement. With two Game Changer windows per match supercharging the scoring, the league's forwards have more opportunities than almost anywhere else — but they still have to take them.

## Highest-scoring match

The single most chaotic game in Baller League UK history came in **Season 1**, when **NDL FC** beat **Gold Devils FC 9-8** — a staggering **17-goal** thriller. It remains the benchmark for just how wild a Baller League match can get when the Game Changers take hold.

## Biggest win

The league's heaviest defeat also arrived in Season 3, when **Prime FC** demolished **Yanited** (now **RTG FC**) **8-1** — a **seven-goal margin** that stands as the most one-sided result in the competition's history.

## Most goals by a team

The all-time leading scorers as a club are **NDL FC**, with **158 goals** across the first three seasons. **SDS FC** (147), **Prime FC** (142) and **Deportrio** (135) are close behind — a reminder that the league's top sides have been consistently prolific.

## What the numbers tell us

Three takeaways stand out:

1. **Goals are the product.** At 7.3 per match, the Baller League is built to entertain. Defensive discipline is valuable, but the league rewards attacking ambition.
2. **The Game Changer moves the needle.** A large chunk of the 1,510 goals have come during the 12th- and 27th-minute rule-change windows, which is where matches are so often won and lost.
3. **Familiar faces dominate.** The same clubs — NDL, SDS, Prime, Deportrio — top the scoring charts, reflecting the consistency of their squads and recruitment.
