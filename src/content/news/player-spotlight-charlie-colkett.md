## Charlie Colkett: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Charlie Colkett** is a midfielder for **VZN FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Chelsea, Swindon Town, Bristol Rovers and Crewe Alexandra player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Charlie Colkett is a left-footed midfielder who came through the highly-rated **Chelsea** academy, where he captained youth teams and was regarded as one of the club's most promising young passers. He went on to play senior football for the likes of **Swindon Town**, **Bristol Rovers** and **Crewe Alexandra**.

A set-piece specialist with excellent vision, Colkett gives VZN a creative heartbeat and a genuine dead-ball threat.

## Career

Charlie Colkett built his professional career at Chelsea, Swindon Town, Bristol Rovers and Crewe Alexandra. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.
