## Speak Baller League

The Baller League has a language of its own, and if you are new to the competition it can feel like a lot to take in. This glossary explains the key terms, rules and concepts you will hear on a matchday — handy whether you are watching at the arena or online.

## The basics

**6v6** — Every match is played six-a-side, including goalkeepers, on a compact indoor pitch. The smaller pitch and fewer players make the game fast and technical.

**Matchday / Gameweek** — The league plays one round per week, on a Monday. There are **11 matchdays** in the regular season, with all 12 teams in action each week across six matches.

**Two 15-minute halves** — Each match runs for 30 minutes total, split into two 15-minute halves. There is no traditional added time; the clock is managed by the officials.

**Wildcard** — Each team is allowed a set number of **wildcards** — substitute players brought in for a matchday — giving managers flexibility to rotate or call on guest talent. Season 4 allows **two per team per matchday**.

## The Game Changers

The league's signature feature. At the **12th minute** of the first half and the **27th minute** of the second half, one of six rule changes is activated for a short window. See our full [Game Changers explained](/news/baller-league-game-changers-explained) guide.

**Onside** — All offside rules are suspended, freeing attackers to find space anywhere.

**Plus One** — The game restarts as a 1v1 and adds one outfield player per goal until full numbers return.

**3Play** — The match becomes 3-a-side with a 30-second shot clock.

**1-on-1** — A series of one-on-one duels, each player confined to their own half with a 15-second shot clock.

**The Line** — Goals scored from beyond the marked line count **double**.

**Fairplay** — Any foul, handball or dissent means an **immediate red card** with no replacement.

## The competition

**Final Four** — The playoffs. After 11 matchdays, the **top four** teams reach the Final Four: two semi-finals (1st vs 4th, 2nd vs 3rd) and a Grand Final to decide the champion.

**Pressure Point / EP (Extra Points)** — One **extra point** is awarded to the winning team of the Pressure Point challenge each matchday. EP is added to a team's league total, so it can decide tight table battles. On our [league table](/table), look for the **EP** column.

**Franchise** — Each of the 12 teams is a franchise with a stable identity (and, usually, a famous manager). Some franchises have been rebranded between seasons — for example, **Yanited became RTG FC** and **Rukkas FC became P17 FC**.

**Manager** — Each team is led by a celebrity or footballing figure who picks the squad and directs the team from the touchline.

## Scoring and the table

**Points (Pts)** — A win is worth **3 points**, a draw **1 point**, a loss **0**, with **EP** added on top. The table is sorted by points, then goal difference, then goals scored.

**Goal difference (GD)** — Goals scored minus goals conceded. It is often the tiebreaker that decides playoff qualification.

**Form** — The results of a team's (or player's) most recent matches, usually shown as a run of wins, draws and losses.
