## Marvin Sordell: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Marvin Sordell** is a striker for **Deportrio** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Watford, Bolton Wanderers, Burnley and Colchester United player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Marvin Sordell is a former Premier League and Championship striker who came through the Watford ranks and went on to play for Bolton Wanderers and Burnley. He represented Great Britain at the London 2012 Olympic Games and was capped by England at U21 level.

Quick, direct and sharp in front of goal, Sordell's experience of playing at the very top makes him a dangerous option for Deportrio, particularly in the one-on-one Game Changer scenarios that reward individual quality.

## Career

Marvin Sordell built his professional career at Watford, Bolton Wanderers, Burnley and Colchester United. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.
