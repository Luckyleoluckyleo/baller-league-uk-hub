## The most decorated squad in the league?

When N5 FC lined up their Season 4 squad, they quietly assembled one of the most experienced groups anywhere in Baller League UK. Managed by **Jens Lehmann** — the former Arsenal and Germany goalkeeper, a member of the legendary "Invincibles" side — N5 have paired a world-class coach with a roster full of players who have competed at the top of the English game.

It is a combination that gives N5 a serious claim to being the league's most professional outfit. Here is a look at the names that stand out.

## The manager: Jens Lehmann

Lehmann needs little introduction. A Premier League winner with Arsenal in 2003–04, a Bundesliga champion with Borussia Dortmund and a UEFA Cup winner with Schalke, he also won **61 caps for Germany**. His elite-level experience and no-nonsense approach set the tone for everything N5 do.

## Billy Sharp

The headline name in the squad is **Billy Sharp**, one of the most prolific goalscorers in the history of the English Football League. A club legend at **Sheffield United**, Sharp scored goals for **Scunthorpe United**, **Doncaster Rovers**, **Southampton** and **Leeds United**, was the **Championship's top scorer in 2018–19**, and has scored over 200 career league goals. Read his full [player spotlight](/news/player-spotlight-billy-sharp).

## Nile Ranger

**Nile Ranger** came through at **Newcastle United**, making his first-team debut as a teenager before spells at **Swindon Town**, **Southend United** and **Blackpool**. Powerful and quick, he is a real handful in the 6v6 format. [Spotlight](/players/nile-ranger).

## Joe Bennett

**Joe Bennett** is an experienced left-back who came through at **Middlesbrough** and went on to play for **Aston Villa**, before helping **Cardiff City** win promotion to the Premier League in 2017–18. A quality left foot and a threat from set pieces. [Spotlight](/players/joe-bennett).

## Kieron Freeman

**Kieron Freeman** is a right-back with extensive Championship and League One experience, most notably at **Sheffield United** and with spells at **Nottingham Forest** and **Derby County**. His overlapping runs are made for the 6v6 game. [Spotlight](/players/kieron-freeman).

## Mustapha Carayol

**Mustapha Carayol** is a skilful, pacy winger and a **Gambia international** who played at the top of the Championship for **Middlesbrough**, **Bristol City**, **Huddersfield Town** and **Ipswich Town**. His one-on-one dribbling is tailor-made for the Game Changer windows. [Spotlight](/players/mustapha-carayol).

## Why it matters

Baller League is won and lost in the margins — sharp finishing, composure under pressure and quick decisions when the rules change. A squad that combines a legendary manager with proven professional know-how is a dangerous proposition. If Lehmann can get his ex-pros firing, N5 FC will be a genuine force in Season 4.
