**Under the Fairplay Game Changer, any foul, handball or show of dissent is an immediate red card with no replacement.** Discipline stops being a detail and becomes the whole rule.

It is the least productive rule for goals in the league — but it may be the most decisive for a single team.

## When Fairplay activates

Fairplay is a **second-half Game Changer**, announced at the **27th minute**. Across Seasons 1–3 it has been activated **57 times** — 14.0% of all activations, the lowest share of the six rules.

## Why it barely moves the scoreline

Fairplay does not create space, change the numbers in your favour or reward a particular skill. It punishes an error. Teams that keep their discipline for three minutes simply play normal 6v6 football, and the window passes without incident.

The numbers show exactly that: **39% of Fairplay windows across Seasons 1–3 produced no goals at all** — 22 of 57. Only 3Play (5%) and 1-on-1 (8%) come anywhere near being as quiet.

When it does matter, it matters disproportionately. A red card late in the second half leaves a team short with little time to recover, and no replacement is allowed.

## How teams win Fairplay

- **Do not tackle to stop a counter.** Under this rule, conceding a goal is cheaper than conceding a red card.
- **Keep your hands down.** Handball is an automatic dismissal for the rest of the window.
- **Stay quiet.** Dissent is treated as a dismissal offence, so arguing a decision can cost a player.
