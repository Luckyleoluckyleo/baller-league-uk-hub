## Joe Bennett: Baller League UK Player Spotlight

**Joe Bennett** is a defender for **N5 FC** in Baller League UK Season 4. A former Aston Villa, Cardiff City, Blackburn Rovers and Wigan Athletic player, he brings genuine professional experience to the 6v6 format.

Joe Bennett is an experienced left-back who came through at **Middlesbrough** before joining **Aston Villa**, where he spent several seasons in the Premier League and Championship. A reliable, attacking full-back, he helped **Cardiff City** win promotion to the Premier League in 2017–18.

Bennett brings top-level defensive experience and a quality left foot to N5 FC, where his crossing and set-piece delivery are a real weapon.

## Career

Joe Bennett built his professional career at Aston Villa, Cardiff City, Blackburn Rovers and Wigan Athletic. That grounding in the full-sided game — the positioning, the decision-making and the technical standards — translates neatly to the fast, high-tempo Baller League, where the Game Changers demand quick thinking and composure under pressure.

## Honours & achievements

- Premier League promotion with Cardiff City (2017–18)
