M7 were one of the original Baller League UK franchises, competing in **Season 1** and **Season 2**. They finished 8th in Season 1 and were crowned **regular-season leaders in Season 2**, topping the table on 22 points with a +12 goal difference before the Final Four playoffs.

M7 did not return for Season 3, when the league expanded its lineup and rebranded several franchises. Their history remains part of the Baller League UK record books — including a memorable Season 2 campaign that saw them set the pace across the regular season.
