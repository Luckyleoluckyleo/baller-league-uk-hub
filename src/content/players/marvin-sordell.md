Marvin Sordell is a former Premier League and Championship striker who came through the Watford ranks and went on to play for Bolton Wanderers and Burnley. He represented Great Britain at the London 2012 Olympic Games and was capped by England at U21 level.

Quick, direct and sharp in front of goal, Sordell's experience of playing at the very top makes him a dangerous option for Deportrio, particularly in the one-on-one Game Changer scenarios that reward individual quality.
