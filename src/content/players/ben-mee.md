Ben Mee is a rugged, left-footed centre-back who spent more than a decade in the Premier League with Burnley and Brentford. A Manchester City academy graduate, he captained Burnley for six seasons and became one of the most respected defenders in the English top flight before his retirement in 2026.

His arrival at Deportrio caused a genuine stir in the Baller League world — a 37-year-old ex-Premier League captain stepping into the 6v6 arena. Mee's reading of the game, physicality and leadership make him a marquee addition to Micah Richards' side.
