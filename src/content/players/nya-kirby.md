Nya Kirby is a technically gifted central midfielder who came through the **Crystal Palace** academy. He was part of the **England** squad that won the **U-17 World Cup in 2017**, one of the standout achievements of English youth football.

A composed passer with a good engine, Kirby is the kind of midfielder who makes a team tick — and his ability to keep the ball under pressure is invaluable in the shot-clock Game Changer formats.
