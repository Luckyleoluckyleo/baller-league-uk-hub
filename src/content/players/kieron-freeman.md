Kieron Freeman is a right-back with extensive experience in the Championship and League One. A **Nottingham Forest** academy product, he spent the best years of his career at **Sheffield United**, where he chipped in with goals from full-back, and also turned out for **Derby County**.

His overlapping runs and delivery from wide make him well suited to the 6v6 game, where full-backs are effectively wingers.
