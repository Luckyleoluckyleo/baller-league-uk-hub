Martyn Waghorn is a hard-working, experienced striker whose career has spanned the length of the English and Scottish leagues. A Sunderland academy product, he went on to play for Leicester City, Rangers and Ipswich Town, scoring regularly wherever he went and earning England U21 recognition along the way.

Waghorn's intelligent movement and clinical finishing make him a natural fit for Deportrio's attacking setup. He links play well and is never afraid to shoot — exactly the profile that thrives when the Game Changers open the game up.
